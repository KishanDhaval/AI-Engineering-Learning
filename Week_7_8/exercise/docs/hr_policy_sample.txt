Sample Company HR Policy Handbook (Placeholder for POC)

1. Notice Period
All full-time employees must serve a notice period of 60 days upon
resignation, regardless of role or tenure. Employees with less than six
months of service may serve a reduced notice period of 30 days, subject to
manager approval. During the notice period, employees remain eligible for
all standard benefits and are expected to complete a formal knowledge
transfer with their team.

2. Leave Carry-Forward
Employees accrue 18 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Up to 10
unused leave days may be carried forward into the following calendar year.
Any unused leave beyond that cap is forfeited on December 31st and is not
paid out, except in the case of confirmed medical leave, which is handled
under a separate policy. Carried-forward leave must be used within the
first six months of the new calendar year or it is forfeited.

3. Leave and Notice Period Interaction
Employees serving a notice period may use accrued and carried-forward leave
during that period, subject to manager approval and adequate handover
coverage. Leave taken during the notice period does not extend the notice
period itself. Employees are encouraged to plan leave usage before
submitting a resignation to avoid forfeiting accrued days.

4. Remote Work
Employees may work remotely up to two days per week with manager approval.
Fully remote arrangements require director-level sign-off and are
reviewed quarterly.

5. Probation Period
New hires undergo a 90-day probation period. During probation, the notice
period for resignation or termination is reduced to 15 days.
