# The Man and the Banana

One morning, a man named Ravi was walking through a small village market. He had been working since early morning and was feeling very hungry. 

As he walked past a fruit stall, he noticed a bunch of fresh yellow bananas.

Ravi bought one banana and continued walking. After a few minutes, he sat under a large tree and peeled it. Just as he was about to take his first bite, he noticed a small child sitting nearby. The child looked tired and hungry.

Ravi asked the child if he wanted the banana. The child smiled and accepted it gratefully. Ravi realized that although he had been hungry, sharing the banana had made him feel happier than eating it himself.

The next day, Ravi returned to the same market and bought two bananas. He ate one and gave the other to someone who needed it. From that day onward, Ravi made it a habit to share whatever little he could.

He learned that even something as simple as a banana can become valuable when it is shared with kindness.
